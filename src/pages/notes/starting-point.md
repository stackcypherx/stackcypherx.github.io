## Where this starts

Not at zero. Over a decade in telecom network and service operations — Smartfren, then Telkom
Indonesia, now Infranexia — currently responsible for mobile broadband service operations across
Papua, Maluku, Sulawesi and Kalimantan. CEH since January, Cisco CyberOps Associate since 2021.

What I do not have is *public* evidence of any of it. More than a decade of work that exists only on a CV,
verifiable by nobody who has not worked with me. For a domestic move that is survivable. For the
global market it is the single biggest thing holding me back — and unlike the technical gaps, it is
fixable immediately and for free.

So this site is two things at once: the portfolio that should have existed five years ago, and the
[roadmap](/roadmap) for the specialisation into security and agentic AI.

## What the plan actually has to close

Being honest about the gaps rather than restarting from fundamentals I already have:

- **Demonstrable hands-on skill.** CEH is multiple-choice. What is missing is a practical, pass/fail
  credential and a public body of lab work behind it.
- **Code and automation.** Python, Git, infrastructure as code, CI/CD. Operations experience without
  automation experience is the hard ceiling on international roles.
- **The AI track.** Genuinely from zero, and the fastest-moving of the three.
- **English to C1.** IELTS 6.5 today. 7.0+ is a formal threshold on several visa routes.

## The rules

**One artifact per milestone.** No checkbox on this site gets ticked without a link. Not a course
completion screenshot — a repo, a writeup, a badge, a report. If I cannot produce the link, the work
is not finished.

**One writeup a week, in English, published whether or not it feels ready.** This is the item that
closes the evidence gap, and it is the one that quietly stops first when work gets busy.

**One primary track.** Cybersecurity, at roughly 70% of my hours — it is the closest adjacency to
what I already do, and the network operations background is an advantage there rather than something
to explain away. Networks stays as depth I already have; AI gets deliberate time because the
security of agentic systems is where I think the scarcest work is.

**One certification per quarter, maximum**, and each has to unlock a filter on a job posting I have
actually read. CEH already covers the HR slot, so the next one is practical or it does not happen.

**A minimum viable week I never drop below**: one lab and one paragraph. That is the floor, not the
target. With a full-time operations job and escalations that do not respect evenings, a floor I can
always clear matters more than an ambitious plan I abandon in month seven.

## What "done" looks like in 24 months

- A practical, pass/fail credential — OSCP or PNPT
- Two flagship projects with live URLs, one in the AI × security overlap
- Fifty-plus published writeups
- Three recommendation letters from three different organisations
- One conference or meetup talk delivered
- IELTS 7.0+
- Either an offer, or a specific and diagnosed reason there isn't one

## Why publish this

Accountability that costs nothing. And because recognition — the kind the strongest visa routes want
as evidence — is not assembled in the month you need it. It is a byproduct of having been visibly
useful for a long time. I have been useful for over a decade; I have just not been visible. Starting
that clock today is free, and I should have done it in 2019.

The next entry will be a technical writeup. This one is just the line in the sand.
